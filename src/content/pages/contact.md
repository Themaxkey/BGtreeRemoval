## Get in Touch With Bowling Green Tree Removal

The fastest way to reach us is by phone at (270) 721-7671. For emergencies — a tree on your house, a split trunk, a limb hanging over a driveway — always call rather than using the form, since the phone gets answered around the clock and messages may not be seen right away.

For quotes, scheduled pruning, stump grinding, or clearing work that is not urgent, the contact form works well and we will get back to you. You can also email us at info@bgtreeremoval.com.

## What to Have Ready When You Call

A few details help us give you a useful answer on the first call rather than guessing. It helps to know roughly how tall the tree is and how close it stands to your house, driveway, fence, or power lines. Tell us whether equipment can reach it, or whether the only way in is through a narrow gate or a soft back yard. Let us know if anything has already failed — a limb down, a crack in the trunk, soil lifting around the base — and whether the tree is dead or still leafing out.

Photos are genuinely useful, especially a wide shot showing the whole tree and what is around it. If you can safely take a couple and send them over, we can often give you a much closer estimate before we ever come out.

## Emergencies and Power Lines

If a tree or limb is in contact with a power line, call your electric provider first — Bowling Green Municipal Utilities in the city, or Warren RECC across much of the rest of the county. No tree service should be cutting on an energized line. Once the utility has made it safe, call us and we will handle the tree. Until then, treat every downed line as live and keep well clear.

## What Happens After You Get in Touch

For non-urgent work we will arrange a time to come out and look at the tree. That visit is free, and you get a price and a scope before anything is booked in. For emergencies we skip straight to working out how quickly we can get a crew to you.

You do not need to be home for most jobs once the scope is agreed, provided we can get access. Moving cars off the driveway and unlocking any gates ahead of time makes a real difference to how quickly the work goes.

## How Quickly We Can Get to You

Scheduling depends heavily on the season. In the quieter months we can usually reach non-urgent work within a few days. After a line of storms crosses Warren County, every crew in the area is committed and planned jobs slide while emergencies get dealt with first.

Genuine emergencies — a tree on a structure, a split trunk, a limb hanging over somewhere people walk — are handled ahead of scheduled work, day or night. If you want pruning or a stump ground and you are not in a rush, booking outside storm season generally means both a faster start and a better price.

## A Note on Quotes

We will not give you a firm price over the phone for a tree we have not seen. Two trees of identical height can differ enormously depending on access, what sits underneath them, and the condition of the wood. What we can do on the phone is give you a sensible ballpark and tell you what would push it up or down — and photos help a great deal with that.

If the honest answer is that your tree does not need work at all, we will tell you that too.
