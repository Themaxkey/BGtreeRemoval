Access is frequently the real problem: steep, wooded lots where no truck can get close mean the tree is climbed, rigged, lowered in pieces and carried uphill to wherever the chipper can sit.

If you have lakeside trees you are unsure about, the things worth watching are exposed roots where the bank has cut back, soil cracking on the uphill side of the trunk, and any change in lean between seasons.
