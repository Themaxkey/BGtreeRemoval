## The December 2021 Tornado

Bristow is one of the places where the December 2021 outbreak left its mark. The second tornado of that morning, an EF2 that touched down in southeastern Bowling Green, tracked northeast roughly parallel to I-65 and caused EF1 damage along McGinnis Road and Bristow Road before dissipating.

Damage from an event like that does not all show up at once. Trees that survived with cracked unions, partially lifted root plates or torn limb attachments often stand for years afterward and then fail in a much more ordinary storm. If you have a large tree that came through 2021 and has looked slightly wrong since, it is worth having someone assess it properly.
