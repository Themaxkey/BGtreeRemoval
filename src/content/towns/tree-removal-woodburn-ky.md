Woodburn lies about eleven miles south-southwest of Bowling Green along US 31W, the old Nashville Road. It is a small city of roughly 300 people that began life as a stagecoach stop and was platted around the railroad in the 1860s. Its name comes from a nearby forest that burned, which is a fitting bit of history for a town where tree work is mostly about what grows in open country.

## Tree Removal in Woodburn, KY

Woodburn sits in the flat southwestern corner of Warren County, and that flatness shapes the work. Trees here are typically farmyard specimens, windbreak rows, fence-line growth and shade trees around older homesteads rather than dense woodland. Access is generally good, which keeps removals efficient, but exposure is the trade-off: a tree standing alone in open ground has taken wind from every direction its whole life and often shows it in a lopsided canopy or a lean that has slowly worsened.
