Richardsville sits roughly nine miles north of Bowling Green along KY 185, in the stretch of northern Warren County that lies between the Barren River and the Green River. It is an unincorporated community with a post office dating to 1872, named for an early settler, and it occupies the most rugged ground of anywhere we work.

## Tree Removal in Richardsville, KY

Northern Warren County is genuinely different from the rest of the area. Where the south and southwest of the county are flat farmland, the land between the rivers is hilly and heavily wooded, with sandstone bluffs, steep hollows and thin soil over rock. It is attractive country and it is difficult country to work in.

## Slope Changes Everything

On sloping ground the whole job changes. Equipment often cannot reach the tree at all, which means climbing and rigging rather than using a bucket. Material has to be carried or winched uphill to wherever the truck can sit. Felling direction is constrained by the slope rather than chosen freely, and anything cut above you moves faster and further than it would on the flat.

None of that is a problem, but it is why a tree in a Richardsville hollow and an identical tree in an open Woodburn field are not the same job or the same price. When you call, the most useful things you can tell us are how far the tree is from somewhere a truck can park, and whether the ground between the two is drivable.

## Wooded Property and Storm Damage

Heavily wooded property brings its own pattern of failure. Trees grown in close competition are tall and slender with small canopies, and when one comes down it frequently brings neighbors down with it or ends up hung up in the canopy of another tree. Hung-up trees are among the most dangerous situations in this line of work and are not something to tackle yourself.
